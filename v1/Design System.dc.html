01

Brand
interpretation

Three behaviours are extracted from the mark and used structurally — never as decoration.

A — Modular field

The 3×3 module is the ancestor of the 12-column grid: 12 = 3 × 4. Every layout division is a multiple of three.

B — State module

A filled module means active. It is the only permitted use of saturated turquoise in UI chrome.

C — Interruption

The diagonal is fixed at −45°, always travelling bottom-left to top-right. It is used for masks, wipes and page transitions — and it is the one element allowed to leave the grid. Because it carries the identity, it does not mirror in RTL.
